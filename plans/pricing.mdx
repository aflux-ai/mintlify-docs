Aflux uses flat, module-inclusive pricing — every plan gives you access to Bulk Email, E-Signature, and QR Codes. There are no per-user seat fees and no hidden add-ons for core features. You pay one price for the whole platform, and you scale by upgrading your plan when your usage or team grows.

<Note>
**Pro and Business plans are coming soon.** The Free plan is available today. Enterprise is available by contacting sales. Pro and Business are currently in development and will be announced on the [Aflux website](https://aflux.io/en/) when they launch.
</Note>

## Changing Your Plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from within the Aflux dashboard:

1. Log in to [app.aflux.io](https://app.aflux.io).
2. Navigate to **Settings → Billing**.
3. Click **Change Plan** and select the tier you want.
4. Confirm the change — upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle.

<Warning>
Downgrading to a lower plan may reduce your usage limits. If your current usage exceeds the limits of the lower plan (for example, more than 5 active QR codes on the Free plan), you will be prompted to archive or delete items before the downgrade can be confirmed.
</Warning>
