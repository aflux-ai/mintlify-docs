## What Aflux does

Aflux has three core modules — Bulk Email, E-Signature, and QR Codes — each designed to handle a specific document workflow while sharing a common storage layer and team management system.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Bulk Email" icon="paper-plane">
    Import contacts, design campaigns with a drag-and-drop editor, schedule sends, and track opens, clicks, and bounces in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="E-Signature" icon="pen-nib">
    Upload a PDF, place signature fields, invite signers, and get a tamper-evident signed document with a full audit trail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="QR Codes" icon="qrcode">
    Create branded QR codes for files or folders, control who can scan them, and monitor scan analytics in your dashboard.
  </Card>

## How Aflux is different

- **Your storage, your rules** — Connect Google Drive (OneDrive and Dropbox coming soon). Your files never move to Aflux's servers.
- **All modules, every plan** — E-signatures, bulk email, and QR codes are included at every tier. No add-ons or per-feature upsells.
- **Flat, transparent pricing** — No per-user penalties. Start free and upgrade when you're ready.
- **EU-based, GDPR-first** — Built by Atek Software, an EU company operating since 2004.

## Where to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up for free](https://app.aflux.io/register) — no credit card required. Your free plan includes e-signing tasks, QR codes, and email sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your storage">
    Link Google Drive to keep your documents in your own cloud. OneDrive and Dropbox support is coming soon. See [Connect Storage](/getting-started/connect-storage).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a module">
    Pick the workflow you need first — send a campaign, request a signature, or generate a QR code. Each module works independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add team members and assign roles. See [Team Management](/platform/team-management) for role options.
  </Step>
</Steps>
