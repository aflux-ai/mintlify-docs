Aflux is an AI media buyer for Telegram. You describe what you are advertising; Aflux finds channels whose audience fits, contacts each channel owner, negotiates the price and the slot, publishes the post, and measures what it delivered.

You keep the controls at every step. Nothing is booked and no money leaves your balance until you approve it.

## How a campaign runs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    A project describes what you sell. Aflux reads your site and drafts the business context every later step reasons from. See [Projects](/guides/projects).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the ad copy">
    Write it yourself or generate it, then validate. See [Ad copy](/guides/ad-copy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the channels">
    Run a search, strike off what you do not want, and keep the `matchToken` the search returns. See [Finding channels](/guides/finding-channels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Set the budget, the date window and the daily spread. The whole budget is committed at this point. See [Campaigns](/guides/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the deals">
    Each channel becomes a placement. The agent negotiates; you approve or cancel each one. See [Placements](/guides/placements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Views, reactions, clicks and cost per click, per placement and per campaign. See [Analytics](/guides/analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>
