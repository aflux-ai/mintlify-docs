Aflux lets you build a team around your document workflows — inviting colleagues, assigning appropriate roles, and controlling exactly which modules each person can access. Whether you need a full admin who manages the account or a guest collaborator who can only view a specific signature request, this guide covers how to set it all up.

## User Roles

Every Aflux account has three built-in roles. Assign the role that matches what each person actually needs — fewer permissions reduce the risk of accidental changes.

| Permission | Admin | Member | Guest |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Manage billing and plan | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Invite and remove users | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Manage integrations and API keys | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Configure account-level settings | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Create and send email campaigns | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Create and send signature requests | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Create and manage QR codes | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Upload and manage files | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| View documents shared with them | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Sign documents as a recipient | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View activity timeline | ✅ | ✅ (own actions) | ❌ |

<Info>
  There is always at least one Admin on every account. You cannot remove or demote the last Admin. To hand off admin rights, promote another member to Admin first.
</Info>

## User Limits per Plan

The number of seats available on your account depends on your plan:

| Plan | Maximum Users |
|---|---|
| Free | 3 users |
| Pro | 20 users |
| Business | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

When you reach your seat limit, the **Invite Member** button is disabled until you upgrade or remove an existing user.

## Inviting a User

Add a new team member from **Settings → Team**. You need Admin rights to invite users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team settings">
    Click your account avatar in the bottom-left corner, select **Settings**, then choose **Team** from the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right of the team list. A dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the user's email address">
    Type the invitee's work email address. You can enter multiple addresses separated by commas to send bulk invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select **Admin**, **Member**, or **Guest** from the role dropdown. You can change this at any time after the invitation is accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. Aflux emails an invitation link to the address you entered. The link expires after 7 days — resend it from the pending invitations list if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm acceptance">
    Once the invitee accepts and creates (or logs into) their Aflux account, they appear in the **Active Members** list and can start working immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Per-Module Access Control

On Pro and above, you can restrict which modules each individual Member or Guest can access — even if their role would normally allow it.

To configure module access for a user:

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click the user's name.
2. Under **Module Access**, toggle access to **Bulk Email**, **E-Signature**, and **QR Codes** independently.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

A Member with Bulk Email toggled off sees no email-related options in the sidebar or dashboard and cannot create or view campaigns.

<Note>
  Per-module access control is available on Pro and above. On the Free plan, all Members have access to all modules by default.
</Note>

## User Groups

On the Business plan and above, you can create named groups (for example, *Marketing Team* or *Legal Department*) and assign module access at the group level instead of per user.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a group">
    Go to **Settings → Team → Groups** and click **New Group**. Give the group a descriptive name and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members to the group">
    From the group's detail page, click **Add Members** and select users from your team list. A user can belong to multiple groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set module access for the group">
    Toggle **Bulk Email**, **E-Signature**, and **QR Codes** on or off for the group. This overrides any individual module access settings for members of the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the group">
    Click **Save Group**. Module access takes effect immediately for all members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use groups when you on-board batches of users who all need the same access profile. Updating module permissions for the group applies to every member at once.
</Tip>

## Guest Accounts

Guests are external collaborators — clients, contractors, or partners — who need limited access to specific documents without full platform access. Guest accounts are available on Pro and above.

Guests can:
- View documents explicitly shared with them
- Sign signature requests they have been added to as a recipient
- Download completed signed PDFs from their dashboard

Guests **cannot** create campaigns, generate QR codes, browse your file storage, or view the activity timeline.

To invite a guest, follow the same [invitation steps](#inviting-a-user) above and select **Guest** as the role.

## Removing or Deactivating Members

**To remove a member permanently:**

1. Go to **Settings → Team**, find the member in the list, and click the **⋯** menu next to their name.
2. Select **Remove Member**. Confirm the action in the dialog.
3. The member loses access immediately. Their previously created documents, campaigns, and QR codes remain in the account.

**To deactivate a member temporarily** (preserving their seat for reactivation):

1. Click **⋯** next to the member's name and select **Deactivate**.
2. The member cannot log in but their data and settings are preserved.
3. Reactivate at any time by clicking **⋯ → Reactivate Member**.

<Warning>
  Removing a member is irreversible — they must be re-invited to regain access. If there is any chance you need to restore access later, use **Deactivate** instead.
</Warning>

## Single Sign-On (SSO)

<Note>
  SSO (SAML 2.0 and OIDC) is an Enterprise-only feature. Contact your Aflux account manager or email support to configure SSO for your organization. Once enabled, team members authenticate through your identity provider (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.) and are provisioned automatically on first login.
</Note>
