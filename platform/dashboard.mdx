The Aflux dashboard is your command center. Every time you log in at [app.aflux.io](https://app.aflux.io), you land here first — seeing live metrics across all three modules, a running timeline of document activity, and shortcuts to start new work without navigating away. This page explains everything the dashboard surfaces and how to make it work for your team.

## Summary Cards

At the top of the dashboard, four summary cards give you an at-a-glance health check of your account:

<CardGroup cols={4}>
  <Card title="Emails Sent" icon="envelope">
    Total emails delivered in the current billing period, with a trend indicator compared to the previous period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signatures Pending" icon="pen-nib">
    Count of open signature requests awaiting at least one signer action right now.
  </Card>
  <Card title="QR Scans" icon="qrcode">
    Cumulative scans across all active QR codes in the current period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Storage Used" icon="hard-drive">
    Your current Aflux storage usage against your plan's quota.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Click any card to jump directly into the relevant module view filtered to that metric — for example, clicking **Signatures Pending** opens the E-Signature list pre-filtered to requests in a pending state.

## Module Shortcuts

Below the summary cards, the dashboard displays a quick-action bar so you can start work in a single click:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="New Email Campaign" icon="paper-plane" href="https://app.aflux.io">
    Opens the Bulk Email editor with a blank campaign ready for your content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Request a Signature" icon="signature" href="https://app.aflux.io">
    Launches the E-Signature document upload flow with recipient configuration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Generate a QR Code" icon="qrcode" href="https://app.aflux.io">
    Opens the QR Code builder to create a new code linked to a file or URL.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

These shortcuts bypass the sidebar and take you directly to the creation step, skipping any list or index views.

## Activity Timeline

The activity timeline sits in the main body of the dashboard and records every meaningful action taken across your account. Each entry shows:

- **Timestamp** — date and time of the action, displayed in your account's configured timezone
- **Actor** — the user or system process that triggered the event (e.g., a team member name, or "Aflux System" for automated events)
- **Event description** — a plain-language summary such as *"Ana Ruiz signed Contract_Q2.pdf"* or *"Campaign 'June Newsletter' delivered to 2,340 contacts"*
- **Module tag** — a color-coded label indicating whether the event belongs to Bulk Email, E-Signature, or QR Codes

The timeline shows your most recent 20 events by default. Click **View full timeline** to open the complete, filterable audit log — see [Notifications and Activity Timeline](/platform/notifications) for details on filtering and retention.

<Note>
  The activity timeline is visible to all team members, but entries are scoped to documents and campaigns each member has access to. Admins see all activity across the account.
</Note>

## Notification Center

The bell icon (🔔) in the top navigation bar opens the notification center — a live feed of alerts that require your attention. Notifications appear here for events including:

- A signer completing or declining a signature request
- An email campaign recording bounces above a threshold
- A QR code exceeding a scan limit or expiring
- Storage quota approaching its plan limit

Unread notifications are counted on the bell icon. Click any notification to navigate directly to the relevant document, campaign, or QR code. Mark individual notifications as read, or use **Mark all as read** to clear the count.

To configure which events trigger alerts and whether they also send an email, go to **Settings → Notifications**. See [Notifications and Activity Timeline](/platform/notifications) for the full configuration guide.

## Switching Module Views

The dashboard supports a condensed per-module view so you can focus on one area without leaving the overview context. Use the **View** toggle in the dashboard header to switch between:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="All Modules">
    The default unified view showing summary cards, shortcuts, and the combined activity timeline for all three modules.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bulk Email">
    Filters summary cards and the activity timeline to email-related events only. Shows campaign delivery rates and recent bounce alerts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="E-Signature">
    Filters to signature events only. Shows pending signer count, completed signatures in the current period, and recent audit activity.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="QR Codes">
    Filters to QR code events. Shows total active codes, scans today vs. yesterday, and any codes that are nearing expiration or scan limits.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Switching module views does not navigate away from the dashboard — it updates the cards and timeline in place.

## Customizing the Dashboard Layout

You can choose which widgets appear on your dashboard to reduce noise and surface what matters most to your workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open layout settings">
    Click the **Customize** button in the top-right corner of the dashboard. A widget panel slides in from the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle widgets on or off">
    Each widget — Summary Cards, Module Shortcuts, Activity Timeline, and Notification Feed — has an individual toggle. Disable any widget you do not need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder widgets">
    Drag widgets in the panel to change the order in which they appear on the dashboard. The preview updates in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your layout">
    Click **Save Layout**. Your preference is stored per user, so each team member can configure their own view independently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you primarily use one module, switch to that module's filtered view and save your layout with only the relevant widgets enabled — this keeps your dashboard fast and focused.
</Tip>
