The Bulk Email module lets you design, personalize, and deliver mass email campaigns — all from the Aflux dashboard. Whether you're a small business sending monthly newsletters or a marketing team running high-volume automated sequences, Bulk Email gives you the contact management, editor tooling, analytics, and compliance controls you need to run professional campaigns without leaving your document workflow.

## Personalization

Generic email gets ignored. Aflux lets you address each recipient by name, adapt content based on their data, and conditionally show or hide entire sections.

<Tip>
  Use merge tags in your subject line or body to insert contact-specific data. The syntax is double curly braces: `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{company}}`, or any custom field you've added to your contact record. If a value is missing, you can define a fallback — for example, `{{firstName | "there"}}` renders as "there" when the first name is blank.
</Tip>

**Dynamic content blocks** let you swap out an entire image, heading, or call-to-action based on a segment the recipient belongs to. **Conditional sections** go further — you can show a block only `if country == "DE"` or hide a promo only for existing customers, all configured in the editor without code.

## GDPR Compliance

Aflux is built by an EU-based company and treats compliance as a first-class feature, not an afterthought.

- **Automatic unsubscribe**: every campaign includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Opt-outs are honoured instantly and cannot be overridden.
- **Suppression lists**: bounced and unsubscribed addresses are stored in a suppression list that prevents accidental re-send.
- **SPF / DKIM / DMARC**: Aflux configures sender authentication on your behalf so your emails pass modern spam filters and inbox placement is maximised.
- **Data residency**: all contact data is stored within the EU on infrastructure that meets GDPR Article 28 processor requirements.

## How to Create Your First Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Bulk Email → New Campaign">
    Open the [Aflux dashboard](https://app.aflux.io) and click **Bulk Email** in the left sidebar. Then click the **New Campaign** button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a campaign name and subject line">
    Give your campaign an internal name (only visible to your team) and write the subject line your recipients will see. You can use merge tags in the subject line, such as `{{firstName}}`, to personalise it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or build a template in the editor">
    Select a template from the library or start from a blank canvas. Drag blocks from the left panel into your layout, then click any block to edit its content, colours, and spacing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select or import your contact list">
    Click **Select Audience** and choose an existing segment or contact list. To use a new list, click **Import Contacts**, upload your CSV or Excel file, and map the columns before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on desktop and mobile">
    Use the **Preview** toggle at the top of the editor to switch between desktop and mobile views. Send a test email to yourself to verify personalisation and rendering in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or send immediately">
    Click **Next: Schedule**. Choose **Send Now** to dispatch immediately or **Schedule** to pick a future date and time. Enable **Timezone Optimisation** if you want Aflux to deliver at the best local time for each recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor results in the Analytics tab">
    Once sending begins, open the **Analytics** tab inside your campaign to watch opens, clicks, and bounces accumulate in real time. Click **Export** at any time to download a full report.
  </Step>
</Steps>
