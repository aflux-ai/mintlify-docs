The E-Signature module lets you request, collect, and verify legally binding signatures on any PDF document — without printing, scanning, or chasing signers by phone. Signatures collected through Aflux are legally recognised in 190+ countries and fully compatible with the EU's eIDAS regulation, making them suitable for contracts, NDAs, consent forms, HR documents, and beyond. Signers need no account or app — they sign directly in their browser on any device.

## Signature Types

Aflux supports four signature methods so every signer can choose the approach that suits them best.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Typed">
    The signer types their full name and Aflux renders it in a signature-style font. This is the fastest option and requires no drawing or file upload. Typed signatures are legally equivalent to drawn signatures under eIDAS and most international electronic signature laws.

    **How it works:** the signer clicks the signature field, types their name, optionally selects a font style, and clicks **Apply**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Drawn">
    The signer draws their signature freehand using a mouse, trackpad, or finger on a touchscreen. The drawing is captured as a vector path and embedded directly into the signed PDF.

    **How it works:** the signer clicks the signature field, draws their signature on the on-screen canvas, and clicks **Apply**. Touch devices display a full-screen canvas for comfort.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Uploaded">
    The signer uploads an image of their handwritten signature (PNG or JPG). Aflux removes the background and scales the image to fit the signature field cleanly.

    **How it works:** the signer clicks the signature field, clicks **Upload Image**, selects a file from their device, and clicks **Apply**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Qualified Electronic Signatures (QES)** — the highest level under eIDAS, requiring identity verification via a digital certificate — are available on **Business and Enterprise plans only**. QES is required for certain regulated industries and high-value contracts in the EU. Contact [support@aflux.io](mailto:support@aflux.io) for certificate provider options.
</Note>

## Multi-Signer Workflows

When a document requires more than one signer, Aflux gives you precise control over who signs in what order and under what conditions.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sequential Signing" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Signers receive the document one at a time in a defined order. Signer 2 is only notified after Signer 1 completes. Ideal for approval chains and hierarchical sign-offs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parallel Signing" icon="arrows-split-up-and-left">
    All signers receive the document simultaneously and can sign in any order. The document completes as soon as the final signer finishes. Best for peer agreements and multi-party contracts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conditional Routing" icon="code-branch">
    Advance the document to different signers depending on field values filled in by earlier signers. Build dynamic approval flows without manual intervention.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Document Templates

Reusable templates save time when you send the same document structure repeatedly — employment contracts, NDAs, order forms, and so on.

- **Drag-and-drop field placement**: position signature, initial, date, checkbox, and freetext fields anywhere on the PDF canvas.
- **Auto-fill**: map template fields to contact data so recipient details are pre-populated when you create a new request from the template.
- **Field validation**: mark fields as required or optional, set character limits, and restrict date fields to past or future dates.
- **Role-based placeholders**: define generic roles (e.g. "Employee", "Manager") in the template and assign real people when you send each request.

## Audit Trail

Every signature request generates an immutable audit trail stored alongside the completed document.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Timestamped Events" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Every action — document sent, viewed, field filled, signature applied, document completed or declined — is logged with a UTC timestamp accurate to the second.
  </Card>
  <Card title="IP & Device Tracking" icon="location-dot">
    Each event records the signer's IP address, browser, and device type, providing evidence of who accessed the document and from where.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Authentication Log" icon="key">
    If you require email OTP or SMS verification before signing, those authentication events appear in the trail alongside the signature events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Certificate of Completion" icon="certificate">
    When all signers have signed, Aflux generates a tamper-evident PDF certificate of completion that embeds the full audit trail and can be submitted as legal evidence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mobile Signing

Signers don't need to install anything. The Aflux signing experience is a responsive web page that opens directly from the email link.

- **No app required**: works in any modern mobile browser — Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Samsung Internet.
- **Touch-optimised drawing**: the drawn signature canvas expands to full screen on small devices so signers can write naturally with their finger.
- **Auto-scroll to fields**: the interface guides each signer through every required field in sequence, so no fields are accidentally missed.

## How to Request a Signature

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to E-Signature → New Request">
    Open the [Aflux dashboard](https://app.aflux.io) and click **E-Signature** in the left sidebar. Then click **New Request** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your PDF or choose a template">
    Drag your PDF onto the upload area or click **Browse** to select it from your device or connected cloud storage. To reuse a configured layout, click **Use Template** and select from your saved templates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place signature, initial, date, and text fields">
    The PDF opens in the field-placement editor. Drag field types from the right panel — **Signature**, **Initials**, **Date**, **Text**, **Checkbox** — onto the pages where you need them. Resize and reposition each field by dragging its handles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add signers — enter names and email addresses">
    Click **Add Signer** and enter each person's name and email address. Assign each field on the document to the appropriate signer using the signer colour-coding in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set signing order (sequential or parallel)">
    In the **Signing Order** panel, drag signers into your preferred order for sequential signing, or toggle **Parallel** to notify all signers at the same time. Add a personalised message for each signer if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send — signers receive a secure email link">
    Review the summary and click **Send Request**. Each signer receives an email with a unique, tamper-proof link to their signing session. No account creation is required on their end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress; download the completed document">
    Return to the **E-Signature** dashboard at any time to see which signers have completed their step and which are pending. Once all parties have signed, the completed PDF and certificate of completion are available to download from the request detail page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
