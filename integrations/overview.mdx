Aflux is built around a bring-your-own storage model — your files stay in the cloud provider you already use, and Aflux connects to them directly without copying or migrating anything to Aflux servers. Whether you send a bulk email campaign, collect e-signatures, or attach a document to a QR code, you always work with the original file sitting in your own storage.

## Why Bring-Your-Own Storage?

Most document platforms silently copy your files into their own storage, creating duplicates you can't manage and raising questions about who actually owns your data. Aflux takes a different approach:

- **Files stay yours.** Aflux reads and writes to your storage provider via a secure OAuth connection. Nothing is copied to Aflux infrastructure.
- **No migration required.** Connect an existing Google Drive folder and start using your files immediately — no upload step.
- **Real-time sync.** Because Aflux always references the live file in your storage, any update you make in Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox is reflected instantly across all Aflux modules.
- **Disconnect at any time.** Revoking the Aflux connection from your storage provider immediately removes Aflux's access. Your files are unaffected.

## How Integrations Work

When you connect a storage provider, Aflux requests a limited OAuth token that allows it to read and write only the files and folders you explicitly grant access to. Aflux never stores your OAuth credentials — only a short-lived refresh token used to maintain the connection. All traffic between Aflux and your storage provider is encrypted over TLS.

Once connected, a storage provider appears as a file source inside each Aflux module. You can browse folders, select files, and attach them to email campaigns, signature requests, or QR codes without leaving the Aflux dashboard.

## Connection Limits by Plan

The number of storage integrations you can connect simultaneously depends on your plan:

| Plan | Connected integrations |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Business | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

<Note>
Internal Storage is always available on every plan and does not count toward your integration limit.
</Note>
