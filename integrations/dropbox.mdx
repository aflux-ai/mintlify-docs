Dropbox is a popular choice for teams that store, share, and collaborate on documents across devices. Aflux is building a native Dropbox integration so you can bring your Dropbox files into Bulk Email, E-Signature, and QR Codes — without moving them out of Dropbox or duplicating them on Aflux servers.

<Info>
**Dropbox integration is not yet available.** The feature is currently in development. You can join the waitlist by emailing [support@aflux.io](mailto:support@aflux.io) with the subject line **Dropbox waitlist**, and the team will notify you as soon as it launches.
</Info>

## What Dropbox Integration Will Do

When the integration launches, connecting Dropbox to Aflux will let you:

- **Browse Dropbox from inside Aflux** — a built-in file picker will let you navigate your Dropbox folders and Dropbox Business team folders without leaving the Aflux dashboard.
- **Attach Dropbox files to email campaigns** — reference files directly in Bulk Email; recipients receive a trackable link to the live Dropbox file without you needing to upload a copy.
- **Send Dropbox PDFs for e-signing** — select a PDF from Dropbox as the source document for a signature request; the completed, signed PDF will be saved back to the originating Dropbox folder automatically.
- **Point QR codes at Dropbox files or folders** — set any QR code destination to a Dropbox item; Aflux resolves the link at scan time, so you can replace the underlying file without regenerating the QR code.

The integration will use Dropbox OAuth, requesting access only to the specific files and folders you choose — not your entire Dropbox account.
