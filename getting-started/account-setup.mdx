Before you can send your first bulk email, request a signature, or generate a QR code, you need an Aflux account. This page walks you through registration, email verification, and the essential profile settings that personalise your workspace — so everything is ready when you bring your team on board.

## Create Your Account

Signing up is free and takes less than two minutes. Aflux does not require a credit card for the Free plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the registration page">
    Open [https://app.aflux.io/register](https://app.aflux.io/register) in your browser. You can sign up with an email address and password, or use a supported OAuth provider if one is available for your organisation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your **full name**, **work email address**, and choose a strong password. Your email address becomes your login identifier and the address Aflux uses for account notifications.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept the terms and register">
    Review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, tick the checkbox to confirm you agree, then click **Create Account**. Aflux will immediately send a verification email to the address you provided.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Open the verification email from Aflux and click the **Verify Email** button inside it. The link is valid for 24 hours. Once clicked, your account is activated and you are redirected to the Aflux dashboard.

    <Note>
      If the email does not appear within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can request a new verification link from the login screen by clicking **Resend verification email**.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the onboarding checklist">
    On your first login, Aflux presents a short onboarding checklist. Work through it to set your profile, connect storage, and explore each module. You can dismiss it at any time and return to it from the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Your Profile

Your profile details appear on documents, signature requests, and email footers, so it is worth filling them in accurately before you start sending anything.

Navigate to **Settings → Profile** to access the following fields:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Name & Display Name" icon="user">
    Your full name is used in system communications. Your display name is what teammates and document recipients see.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Company Name" icon="building">
    Aflux surfaces your company name on signature certificates and email footers. Keep it consistent with your brand.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timezone" icon="clock">
    Set your local timezone so that scheduled emails and signature deadlines are calculated correctly for your location.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Profile Photo" icon="image">
    Upload a photo or logo. It appears in the dashboard sidebar, on team management screens, and in audit trails.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

After updating each field, click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the profile form.

## Set a Secure Password

If you registered with an email and password (rather than OAuth), you can update your password at any time under **Settings → Security → Change Password**. Choose a password that is at least 12 characters long and unique to Aflux. Aflux transmits all credentials over TLS and stores passwords using industry-standard hashing.

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication

<Note>
  Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on the **Pro plan and above**. If you are on the Free plan, upgrade to Pro to unlock this feature. Visit **Settings → Billing** to view available plans.
</Note>

Two-factor authentication adds a second verification step every time you sign in, protecting your account even if your password is compromised. To enable it:

1. Go to **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Click **Enable 2FA** and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password.
3. Enter the six-digit code shown in your authenticator app to confirm the setup.
4. Save your backup codes somewhere secure — these let you recover access if you lose your device.

Once enabled, Aflux will prompt you for a verification code on every login.

## Navigate Account Settings

All account-level preferences live under the **Settings** menu in the left sidebar. The sections most relevant when getting started are:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Profile" icon="circle-user">
    Name, display name, company, timezone, and profile photo.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Security" icon="shield-halved">
    Password management and two-factor authentication (Pro+).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    Choose which events trigger email or in-app notifications for your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    Connect cloud storage providers such as Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="credit-card">
    View your current plan, upgrade, and manage payment methods.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="users">
    Invite teammates and assign roles (Admin, Member, or Guest).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
