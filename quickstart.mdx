Aflux brings your entire document workflow — bulk email campaigns, e-signatures, and QR codes — into a single unified platform. This guide walks you through creating a free account, connecting your cloud storage, and completing your very first workflow in under five minutes.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- A free Aflux account — register at [https://app.aflux.io/register](https://app.aflux.io/register)

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the registration page">
    Open [https://app.aflux.io/register](https://app.aflux.io/register) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details and sign up">
    Fill in your full name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Sign Up**. Aflux will send a verification email to the address you provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Open the email from Aflux and click the **Verify Email** link. The link expires after 24 hours, so complete this step promptly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    After verification you are redirected to the Aflux dashboard — your central hub for all document workflows. From here you can access Bulk Email, E-Signature, QR Codes, and platform settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 2: Connect Your Cloud Storage

Aflux uses a bring-your-own-storage model. Connecting a cloud provider lets you access and attach files directly from within every module.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the dashboard, then select **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Drive">
    Under the **Cloud Storage** section, click **Connect** next to **Google Drive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Aflux in the Google OAuth dialog">
    A Google sign-in window opens. Select the Google account you want to link and click **Allow** to grant Aflux read/write access to your Drive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Your Google Drive now appears as a selectable storage source throughout all Aflux modules — including when uploading PDFs for e-signature or attaching assets to email campaigns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **OneDrive and Dropbox integrations are coming soon.** You will be able to connect them from the same **Settings → Integrations** page once they are available.
</Info>

## Step 3: Choose Your First Workflow

Pick the workflow that best matches what you want to accomplish today. Each tab below walks you through the core steps and links to the full module guide.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Send an Email Campaign">
    Reach your contacts with a professionally designed email campaign — all without leaving Aflux.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a campaign">
        Navigate to **Bulk Email → New Campaign**. Give your campaign a name, choose a sender address, and build your email using the drag-and-drop editor or start from a template.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add your contacts">
        Import a CSV file or manually add contacts to a list. Apply personalization tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) to tailor the message to each recipient.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Schedule or send">
        Preview your email, set a send time (or send immediately), and click **Launch Campaign**. Track opens, clicks, and bounces in real time from the campaign analytics dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Ready to go deeper? See the full [Bulk Email guide](/modules/bulk-email).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Request a Signature">
    Collect legally binding e-signatures on any PDF — with a full audit trail and eIDAS compatibility.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Upload your PDF">
        Go to **E-Signature → New Request** and upload the document you need signed. You can pull files directly from your connected Google Drive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add signers and place fields">
        Enter each signer's name and email address. Drag signature, initials, and date fields onto the document for each signer, and choose between a sequential or parallel signing order.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the request">
        Click **Send for Signature**. Each signer receives an email with a secure link. You can monitor the signing status and receive notifications at every step from the E-Signature dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Ready to go deeper? See the full [E-Signature guide](/modules/e-signature).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Generate a QR Code">
    Create dynamic QR codes that link to any file or URL — with scan analytics and full lifecycle control.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select your file or destination">
        Go to **QR Codes → New Code** and choose the file from your connected cloud storage or paste in a target URL.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Customize your code">
        Apply your brand colors, add a logo, set an expiration date or scan limit, and optionally protect the destination with a password.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Download and deploy">
        Click **Generate**, then download your QR code as a PNG or SVG. Share it in print, email, or on the web. Scan activity is tracked in real time on your QR Codes dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Ready to go deeper? See the full [QR Codes guide](/modules/qr-codes).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
